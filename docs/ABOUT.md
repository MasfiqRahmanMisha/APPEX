## LinkedIn caption
🚀 Introducing **Appex** — an Android APK marketplace I built from scratch.

Think Play Store, but small, clean and fully mine:
📱 Users browse, download & review apps
👩‍💻 Developers (invite-only) upload APKs and track downloads & ratings
🛡️ Admins review and approve every release before it goes live

Under the hood: HTML/CSS/JS + Supabase (Postgres, Auth, Storage) with Row Level Security, private APK storage with signed downloads, and a glassmorphism UI, deployed on Vercel.

The part I enjoyed most: designing the database rules so users can't promote themselves, skip approval or fake downloads — enforced in Postgres, not just in the UI.
